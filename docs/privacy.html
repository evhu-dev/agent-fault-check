Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Contact: support@evhu.net

1. Zero CLI Telemetry

The afc and afc-pro CLI binaries run locally in your development or CI environment. They collect no usage telemetry and never transmit your source code, agent prompts, HTTP traffic, or database contents to evhu.

2. License Key Validation

When Pro or Team features are invoked, afc-pro sends only your Polar license key and organization identifier to Polar's Customer Portal API (api.polar.sh) to verify entitlement status.

3. Billing & Payment Data

Purchases are processed by Polar.sh (Merchant of Record). evhu does not receive or store credit card numbers or banking details.

4. Support Communications

If you email support@evhu.net, we use your email address and message solely to respond to your inquiry and provide product support.
